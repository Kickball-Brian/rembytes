Envision Healthcare

Digital Marketer (SEO)

Feb 2020 – May 2021

  • Structured data
  • JobPosting schema
  • Technical SEO
  • Analytics

Putting open roles on the map

A healthcare employer with a constant stream of openings needed its jobs to be understood by search engines, not just read by candidates.

The challenge

Every open role lived on the site as an ordinary page. Search engines had to guess at titles, locations and requirements, which left a steady source of qualified traffic on the table.

What I did

  • Implemented JobPosting structured data so each opening described itself to search engines in a standard, machine-readable way.
  • Paired the rollout with tagging, call tracking and analytics strategy, so the effect on traffic could be measured instead of assumed.

The outcome

Organic traffic rose 50% after the JobPosting markup went live.

  • 50%increase in organic traffic

Reputation at 260-location scale

Hundreds of rooftops and providers, each needing an accurate listing and a reputation worth finding.

The challenge

With more than 260 rooftop and provider locations, listings drift, details go stale and reviews arrive unevenly. At that scale, goodwill does not accumulate on its own.

What I did

  • Managed business listings for every one of the 260+ locations through Reputation.com.
  • Created an automated review request system, so asking for feedback became part of the patient journey instead of an afterthought.

The outcome

Reputation scores for Google Business Listings tripled.

  • 3×Google Business reputation scores
  • 260+locations managed

One modular platform for corporate and clinical sites

Moving a WordPress multisite onto Kentico 12 Xperience, with a template that works like a kit of parts.

The challenge

Corporate and clinical sites each needed to be created, updated and kept healthy, in step with Marketing Communications, and a WordPress multisite had to move to a new platform.

What I did

  • Created corporate and clinical sites on Kentico Portal Engine and shipped updates and features alongside Marketing Communications.
  • Managed the AWS EC2 instance and DNS records behind the sites.
  • Ran comprehensive technical SEO audits, resolved on-site errors and expanded the quality of the corporate backlink infrastructure.
  • Trained content writers on SEO best practices every month.

The outcome

A reusable foundation that let new sites and features be built from shared parts, with SEO knowledge spread across the writing team.
